# Item-level Start/End dates not captured

The assignment spec's Item field list (Product name, Quantity, Price, Total Amount) has no date fields, and neither do CONTEXT.md's Item definition or the `ExtractedItem`/`so_items`/`po_items` shape. All four sample documents' items tables carry a Start Date / End Date column per row nonetheless, and — unlike Burst (ADR-0002) — this isn't a spec artifact with no real data behind it: it carries genuine information the document-level `startDate`/`endDate` Structured Fields don't convey. Most items are annual sub-slices of the overall contract term with per-year pricing (e.g. BrightOps' Analytics Platform line splits into a 03-01-2025–02-28-2026 row at $72,000 and a 03-01-2026–02-28-2027 row at $78,000, inside a document-level term of 03-01-2025–02-28-2027; CloudShield and NovaFleet's core subscription items follow the same pattern), while a few items instead span the full document-level term in a single row (NovaFleet's Advanced Threat Monitoring, ACME's Training Package). Neither shape is reconstructable from the document-level dates alone — this is a real information loss, not a no-op.

We chose not to capture item-level dates anyway, matching the assignment brief and CONTEXT.md's Item shape rather than the raw table shape. If a downstream need shows up for per-item billing periods (revenue recognition by contract year, mid-term item changes, etc.), add `start_date`/`end_date` columns to `so_items`/`po_items` and the corresponding fields to `ExtractedItem`.
